# PHILOSOPHY.md · elevating About + Services to the reference class
Proposal for DJ's approval, 2026-07-07. Nothing here is applied yet; the v3 copy stays locked until DJ signs a direction. References studied live: Ogilvy, Day One, Laundry Service, Belief Agency, W+K (sources at bottom).

## The diagnosis in one line
The site argues taste everywhere except the one page that lists what you sell; an 8-tile service menu is the single most vendor-like element on a site whose thesis is judgment.

## What the reference class actually does (shared principles)
1. Belief before capability. All five lead with a worldview; services come last or barely at all.
2. One idea per shop. Truth (Belief), freshness (Day One), culture (Laundry Service), work-first independence (W+K).
3. The comparison sentence states taste as judgment: "stories > content", "culture-worthy > press-worthy".
4. Services carry opinions; never bare nouns.
5. Withholding signals status: vendors explain, peers assert. BUT terseness is earned by fame; DJ earns it with receipts, so: minimal in tone, never minimal in evidence.
6. People are characters, not resumes ("internet archaeologists", "rebels and dissenters").
7. The work does the enumerating; cases replace capability lists as proof.

## Proposal A · About becomes the creed page (Belief Agency pattern)
Keep: the hero ("I build monuments with my friends."), the subject-file conceit, the receipt, In the room, crew, method, BTS, CTA.
Change: the Short Version becomes THE CREED — 4-5 stacked declarative beliefs, each with one supporting sentence. DJ's locked lines nearly are this already:
- The tools are everywhere. Taste isn't.
- AI is a tool, not a solution.
- Every great story is a human story.
- Less agency, more rock & roll.
- (candidate 5th, his voice to write: something about pressure/showing up, e.g. the "worth remembering" idea)
Order becomes: hero → creed → receipts (the proof the creed cashes out) → In the room → crew → method → giving → BTS → CTA.
Crew framing borrows Day One's insiders/outsiders honesty: one director, a trusted bench, named Tess. Honesty about size IS the senior move; implying headcount is the vendor move.

## Proposal B · Services collapses 8 → 3 belief-led buckets (Ogilvy wrap, W+K open)
Open with the negative definition (W+K shape): "Less agency, more rock & roll." plus one or two "what I don't do" lines (DJ writes; candidates: no decks for the sake of decks, no gear talk, no 40-slide strategy theater).
Then THREE buckets max, each opened by one belief line, with the current 8 offers demoted to small supporting lists inside:
1. **Direction & Brand** (belief line: taste/judgment) — swallows Creative Direction, Direction, Brand Building, Creative Consulting
2. **Campaigns & Content** (belief line: stories > content / built to travel) — swallows Integrated Campaigns, Experiential, Photography
3. **AI Systems** (belief line: "AI is a tool, not a solution.") — offer 08 + link to /ai
Page keeps the ruled-index design system; each bucket row expands or lists its sub-offers in mono. "Production comes standard" line survives as the closer (with Tess introduced properly or moved to About).
Optional rename: "Services" → "Capabilities" (weaker conviction: keep "Services", DJ's call).

- W+K-grade withholding without W+K-grade fame (keep the receipts).
- More than 5 beliefs (manifesto sprawl reads as mood board).
- Borrowed mantras ("fail harder" homages). Structural theft only, never verbal.
