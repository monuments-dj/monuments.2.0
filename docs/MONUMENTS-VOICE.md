## The core of DJ's rules (condensed from the canonical docs)

- Reference point: Donald Miller. Clarity over cleverness. The reader is the smart hero; the studio is the guide. Never over-explain.
- Sounds like: nuanced, a little witty, simple, human. Expert with dry humor. Instagram meets billboard in LA, not LinkedIn.
- Voice: "I" throughout the site. A creative director who directs his own campaigns and films. Never flip to "director who also does CD."
- Never: em dashes. Buzzwords. Agency-speak. Hollow one-liners. Gag or pun headers. Self-mythologizing tough-guy copy.
- Banned phrases on record: "we make brands more human," "most great work starts with an honest conversation," "built to scale," "the best brands don't just make content," "the thinking comes with it," "a decade in the hard rooms."
- The best lines are DJ's own words, cut down. His approved lines end one beat earlier than expected. Trust the reader. Drop the observation.
- Stat rule: nothing in a stat row unless DJ would say it in an interview under questioning. Numbers attributed to the campaign that earned them.

## Session addendum (July 12 work-pages pass, tested against DJ's edits)

- Body copy in case studies is narrative prose in complete sentences. Clipped fragments live only in heroes, stat rows, tiles, tickers.
- The setup-snap payoff ending ("The job was making the city want to wear it. The city wears it.") is exactly the "add the observation" mistake. End a beat earlier: "Walk around Boise now and the jerseys are everywhere."
- Colon-list section beats (The offer: / The wins: / The team:) are a device for ONE page (Art of Visuals), not a house style.
- Never overclaim a role. Name the actual verbs; credit collaborators by name (April Frame, Nathan Zanders, Dawson Gutierrez, Isabel Sarhad). "Helped" and "assisted" as hedges are dead; "helped" is fine when it is literally the claim (helped Adorama land on the name).
- Never shrink a partner to elevate DJ (killed: "the biggest piece of business he'd ever chased").
- The team gets the growth; DJ gets the lane ("The agency is what we built, and the creative was my lane").
- Flag for DJ: Turnstile's brief heading "Crafting a brand that hits all the right notes" is a pun header and likely violates his own rule. His call.

## Killed lines from this session (with reasons, as calibration)

- "I direct talent better because I've been the talent." On the nose; states the moral.
- "The red light taught me more about directing than the monitor ever did." Writerly costume.
- "I grew the agency 4x." Untrue as a solo claim.
- "I helped with creative direction and assisted in execution." Hedge stack, reads junior.
