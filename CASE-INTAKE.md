the /work row + grid card + tags, the home reel card, the hire page, the audit
sheet. No more archaeology. Copy this template per campaign, fill what you know,
leave blanks; blanks render as nothing (never as placeholders that ship).

The one rule that governs everything here: **if you would not say it flatly in
an interview, it does not go on the sheet.**
