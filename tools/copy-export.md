## Story Section — Why We Do It
- \ Why we do it
- A camera is just a tool. What you point it at is the whole decision.
- We are not a foundation and we are not pretending to save the world. We are a studio that happens to be good at making people feel something, and that skill has no business sitting idle when a cause, a kid, or a true story needs it.
- So we set aside real time for work that pays nothing and matters more. No press release, no logo on a banner. Just the work, done right, handed to someone who could not have made it alone.
