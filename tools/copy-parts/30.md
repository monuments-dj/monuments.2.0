## The Brief section
- \ The brief
- Elevating the art of waffles: a brand refresh for Waffle Me Up.
- 01 Brand Identity
- 02 Logo Design
- 03 Photography
- 04 E-Commerce
- 05 Web Design
- Waffle Me Up creates some of the best waffles in the world, and they wanted a brand that captured the passion, quality, and joy they bring to every bite.
- I initially designed their logo when they opened their first storefront, establishing a welcoming look that matched the warmth of their product. Years later, as they grew and needed a brand refresh, they came back to me to reimagine their identity and expand into e-commerce.

## The Work section
- \ The work
- Concept. Design. Deliver.
- Working closely with the team, we took a deep dive into their brand story, focusing on the artistry and care behind each waffle. I designed new, elevated packaging and custom boxes to bring their product to life, from the moment it leaves the store to the first bite at home.
- Then we tackled the digital space, building out a streamlined online store that let them sell and ship waffles directly to customers nationwide. To strengthen the brand's visual identity, I directed and shot a series of editorial-style brand images, plus a behind-the-scenes video showing the hands-on care that goes into every batch.
- Objectives
- 01 Refresh a beloved identity without losing the warmth that made it work.
- 02 Design elevated packaging that carries the experience from store to doorstep.
- 03 Build an e-commerce store so the best waffles in the world could ship nationwide.
