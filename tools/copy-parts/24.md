## Podcast section
- The podcast · Creator Clubhouse
- 300K+ monthly listeners · co-hosted
- Creator Clubhouse · Spotify · Art of Visuals
- Creator Clubhouse.
- Creator Clubhouse is the Art of Visuals podcast, and I co-hosted it every week. We grew it past 300,000 monthly listeners by taking one question seriously: what does it take to be a creative in the modern world? We sat down with CMOs and culture-shifting creatives and got into the parts that never make the highlight reel: mental health, consistency, relationships. Minimal gear talk, maximum humanity.
