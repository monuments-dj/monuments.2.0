## Section: The Idea
- \ The idea
- Merch worth wearing: a meaningful extension of the brand.
- For a range of clients, I've had the chance to design clothing and merch that connects with their brand story, brings their community closer, and feels genuinely wearable. From concept to production, I make sure each piece reflects not just a logo or a message, but a meaningful extension of the brand itself. Whether I'm designing it, printing it, or marketing it, I know what works in the space.
