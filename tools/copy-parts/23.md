## The Challenge Section
- \ The challenge
- The truth, not a lecture.
- A statewide anti-vaping campaign for Idaho Public Television, built to pull teens away from vaping by telling the truth about Big Tobacco instead of wagging a finger. DJ directed the Myth vs Fact spots inside it; the campaign won a 2024 Northwest Regional Emmy.
- Idaho's youth vaping crisis was built by Big Tobacco: addictive, easy to hide, fruity-flavored devices aimed straight at kids. Finger-wagging does not move a teenager.
- So Know Vape did the opposite. The series exposed the chemicals, named the way the industry preys on young people, and separated myth from fact, in a voice teens would actually watch. "Be Smart. Don't Start." Real information, on their level, not adults talking down.

## The Role Section (Paper background)
- \ The role
- Behind the camera.
- DJ directed all six Myth vs Fact spots, the films at the heart of the campaign that carried the message to Idaho teens. Each one had to make a public-health point land in seconds, in a voice a teenager would not scroll past. The work is part of an Emmy-winning Know Vape campaign.

## Campaign Stills Section
- The stills · web, social + OOH
- Shot by DJ · 12 frames
- I shot the campaign, too.
- Directing the spots was only half of it. I also shot the campaign's stills, the frames that ran on the web, across social, and on billboards around Idaho. The brief moved away from fear and lectures toward something truer: real slices of teenage life in Idaho. Practices and portraits, friends and the quiet ordinary moments worth protecting. I photographed them like they mattered, because they do.

## GFX / Brand System Section

## Award Section (Rose background)
- \ Recognition
- Part of an Emmy-winning campaign.
