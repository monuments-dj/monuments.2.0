## The Brief Section
- \ The brief
- Crafting a brand that hits all the right notes.
- When Turnstile Audio came to me, they wanted a brand identity that struck the perfect balance: clean, simple, and inspired by the legendary audio brands of the past, but fresh enough to stand out in today's market. They didn't want flashy. They wanted timeless. Something that communicated quality and craftsmanship without overcomplicating things, and felt welcoming to both up-and-coming creators and seasoned pros alike.
- A name that earns the volume it asks for. Our timeline? Two weeks to design, iterate, and launch.

## The Work Section
- \ The work
- Concept. Design. Deliver.
- Identity first. A single confident wordmark, geometric and built to last just like the gear, paired with a mark that reads from across a loud room. Then the content: product photography shot so the metal and the message hit at the same time.
- Then the campaign. We took a deep dive into the world of vintage audio brands, what made them iconic and enduring, then built something modern and a little nostalgic without trying too hard. The brand quietly amplifies; the gear is the star.
- \ Objectives
- Craft a brand identity that captures Turnstile's ethos of precision, quality, and affordability.
- Design something that feels understated but undeniably cool, a nod to the classics.
- Let the gear be the star; the brand quietly amplifies the message under a two-week timeline.
