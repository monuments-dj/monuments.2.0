## The brief section
- \ The brief
- When the world gets noisy, make quiet desirable.
- Feeling overwhelmed shouldn't be the norm, but it's a feeling we can all relate to. When the world gets noisy, Sony's WH-1000XM4 noise-canceling headphones aren't just a piece of tech, they're a device for creating an environment, a moment of peace and serenity no matter where you are or what may be happening around you. At Art of Visuals, I teamed up with Sony to capture that moment. This moment is for you.
- The film follows three creators we cast because they fit the story naturally: Audrie Storme, Karl Shakur, and Eric Rubens. Each one moves through their own version of a chaotic day, overwhelmed, anxious, frustrated, until the headphones go on. Eric finds his moment alone on a sailboat in the middle of the ocean at sunset. Audrie finds hers at home, surrounded by her music and her art. Karl escapes the city to a rooftop over downtown LA and dances the tension out.

- \ The job
- Shape the story. Hold the standard.
