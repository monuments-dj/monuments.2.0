## My Part section
- \ My part
- A crew of thirty, one vision.
- As the Creative Director and Co-Director of this campaign, my responsibilities included developing the initial pitch, managing a film crew of 30+, and overseeing both video and still photography. This included casting, creative briefs, and helping compose the music. Every aspect of the production had to align with Sony's brand identity and show the product in its best light, from the first frame to final delivery.
- The full package was a hero film, hero imagery, and a gear highlight video, pushed everywhere AOV lives: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, the newsletter, and the podcast. Sony published the film on its own channel and reposted the work on the @sony feed. It pulled 1.6 million impressions and 204 thousand actions, with an engagement rate more than double what the industry considers high.
