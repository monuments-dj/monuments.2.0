## The brief section
- \ The brief
- Make a health brand feel like the people it serves.
- Blue Cross needed a campaign that authentically represented their most valued asset: their customers. The brief was to highlight the diversity and individuality of their customer base in a relatable, human way, with visuals that resonate emotionally by capturing everyday moments in a variety of settings.

## Break section caption

## The job section
- \ The job
- A week on location, person by person.
- In partnership with OMEK agency, I helped with creative direction and assisted in execution of the campaign, from initial concept through final image selection. Over the course of a week, we captured authentic moments in a variety of locations: local shops, homes, and outdoor areas, highlighting the personal connection Blue Cross has with its customers.
