## Story Section — Why We Do It
- \ Why we do it
- A camera is just a tool. What you point it at is the whole decision.
- We are not a foundation and we are not pretending to save the world. We are a studio that happens to be good at making people feel something, and that skill has no business sitting idle when a cause, a kid, or a true story needs it.
- So we set aside real time for work that pays nothing and matters more. No press release, no logo on a banner. Just the work, done right, handed to someone who could not have made it alone.

- Pro-bono creative
- Some of the best stories belong to people who can't afford a campaign. So a few times a year we hand our full toolkit to a cause that has earned it: the strategy, the shoot, the edit, the rollout. Same crew, same standard as the paid work. The only difference is the invoice.

- Mentorship
- Nobody walks onto a set knowing how it works. We did not. So we keep a door open for young makers who have the eye but not the network: time on real projects, honest notes, and the kind of access that usually costs a connection you don't have yet. Talent is everywhere. The room is not.

- Documentary & mission work
- We have carried cameras through Nepal, Uganda, and across Europe for work that was never about selling anything. We still take that work when it matters, and we bring everything that pressure taught us to it.

## Story Section — Close to Home
- \ Close to home
- Boise raised us. Los Angeles made us. We answer to both.
- Most of what we give happens within driving distance of the two places we call home.
- A local nonprofit that needs a film and can't afford one. A high schooler with a camera and no idea where the door is. A neighbor's project that deserves to look as good as it is. You don't have to fly across the world to find work worth doing for free. Usually it's down the street.

## Further Afield Section (Dark Band)
- \ Further afield
- When the story is bigger than us, we go to it. Documentary and mission work in Nepal, Uganda, and Europe.
- We are careful with this kind of work. We go in to listen, not to take. The footage belongs to the people in it as much as it belongs to us, and the goal is never a highlight reel. It's the truth, told with enough care that it lands.
